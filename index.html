NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a transport quote?

Fill out the enquiry form with your pickup and delivery locations, goods details and contact information. The details will be prepared in WhatsApp for you to send.

Which areas do you serve?

STC is based in Kolhapur, Maharashtra. Contact us with your pickup and delivery locations to confirm route availability.

How is the transport price calculated?

Pricing may depend on route, distance, cargo type, weight, vehicle requirement, timing and other shipment details. Request a quote for your specific load.

Can businesses enquire about regular transport?

Yes. Businesses can contact us to discuss recurring loads, expected frequency, routes and vehicle needs.

Is my booking confirmed after submitting the form?

No. The form prepares an enquiry message. Your transport is not booked until availability, price and arrangements have been confirmed directly with the company.
